# TOOL-6: TOOL-6 Checkout

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: The user can finish checkout and see a confirmation.

## TC-1: Customer finishes checkout and sees a confirmation
- Covers: AC1
- Type: positive
- Preconditions: A customer account exists and is logged in. The customer has at least one item in the cart and is able to reach the checkout page.

Steps:
1. Open the cart and start checkout.
2. Complete the checkout flow through to its final step.
3. Submit/finish the checkout.

Expected: Checkout completes and a confirmation is displayed to the user. The exact content and wording of the confirmation is not specified in the story.
